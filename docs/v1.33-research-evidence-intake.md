# TuneForge v1.33 official research evidence intake

## Outcome

Milestone 4 adds a protected, human-controlled boundary between captured
official-web research and TuneForge's reviewed evidence/claim systems. A live
result remains an unreviewed research lead until a reviewer explicitly triages
it. Triage alone creates no Evidence Source, passage, claim, or public fact.

The workflow is:

```text
Official research lead
  -> reviewer triage
  -> evidence intake
  -> separately approved Evidence Source passage
  -> draft Guidance Claim
  -> existing claim review
  -> public fact only after approval
```

## Reviewer workflow

The protected `/trusted-web-research` queue and result detail page expose:

- original query, URL, title, publisher, and retrieval timestamp
- exact captured excerpt and content SHA-256
- research policy version and explicit unreviewed trust tier
- linked source/canonical setting and inferred hardware/BIOS scope
- same-publisher Evidence Sources and immutable passage choices
- related claims, a conservative conflict signal, and append-only history

Reviewer decisions include evidence intake, needs corroboration, low quality,
rejected, and archived. A rejected or archived lead cannot skip directly to
reviewed evidence. Once a lead crosses the reviewed-evidence boundary, its
research context cannot be silently retriaged.

Evidence linking fails closed unless the source is approved, the passage
belongs to that source, and its publisher matches the captured research result.
Creating or extending a claim is limited to drafts for the same canonical
identity. Neither action approves or publishes a claim.
