## Approved decisions

- **Power Down Enable** - 1 mapping from asrock. The ASRock heading contains the documented 'Eanble' transposition, but its official description explicitly enables or disables DDR power-down mode, matching the existing ASUS, GIGABYTE, and MSI identity.
- **HD Audio Controller** - 1 mapping from gigabyte. The GIGABYTE Intel 800 Audio Controller record has the same onboard-audio enable/disable function and wording as the already mapped GIGABYTE record.
- **DMI ASPM** - 1 mapping from asus. ASUS DMI ASPM Control explicitly configures DMI ASPM support with Disabled, Auto, and ASPM L1 choices, matching the existing DMI-link identity.
- **AMD ECO Mode** - 1 mapping from asus. The ASUS Ai Tweaker record adjusts AMD CPU control limits through explicit cTDP profiles, matching the reviewed AMD ECO Mode function.
- **TX Burst Length** - 1 mapping from asus. The ASUS Nitro TX Burst Length record selects the same 1x, 2x, 4x, or 8x DDR write-training pattern length as the existing cross-vendor identity.
- **Read Drift Adjustment** - 2 mappings from asus. ASUS exposes the same read-drift choices for P0 and P0-3 scopes alongside the already mapped generic control.
- **Write Drift Adjustment** - 2 mappings from asus. ASUS exposes the same write-drift choices for P0 and P0-3 scopes alongside the already mapped generic control.
- **DRAM VDD Voltage** - 4 mappings from asus. Four ASUS records explicitly control the DRAM IC VDD portion in synchronized or per-PMIC presentations, matching the reviewed primary DRAM-supply identity.
- **DRAM VDDQ Voltage** - 4 mappings from asus. Four ASUS records explicitly control the DRAM IC data-portion VDDQ rail in synchronized or per-PMIC presentations, matching the reviewed DRAM I/O-supply identity.
- **DRAM VPP Voltage** - 4 mappings from asus. Four ASUS records explicitly control the DRAM activating-power VPP rail in synchronized or per-PMIC presentations, matching the reviewed DRAM VPP identity.
- **Save All Secure Boot Variables** - 1 mapping from msi. The MSI contextual source record saves PK, KEK, db, dbt, and dbx under Key Management, matching the existing ASUS and MSI Secure Boot export identity.
- **Enroll Efi Image** - 1 mapping from msi. The MSI contextual source record enrolls a PE image SHA-256 certificate into db, matching the existing ASRock and MSI identity.
- **PPC Adjustment** - 1 mapping from gigabyte. The GIGABYTE B550 record differs only by an availability-note suffix and explicitly fixes the CPU P-state, matching the reviewed PPC Adjustment identity.
- **Power Supply Idle Control** - 1 mapping from gigabyte. The GIGABYTE B550 record differs only by an availability-note suffix and exposes the same Typical Current Idle, Low Current Idle, and Auto behavior.
- **SATA Controller(s)** - 2 mappings from gigabyte. Two GIGABYTE AMD manuals describe Chipset SATA Port Enable as enabling or disabling the integrated SATA controllers, exactly matching the reviewed SATA-controller identity.
- **PCH 1.05V Voltage** - 1 mapping from asus. ASUS PCH 1.05V Primary Voltage explicitly configures the PCH 1.05 V rail with the same range as the existing ASUS record.
