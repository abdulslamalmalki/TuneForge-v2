# TuneForge v1.29 Platform, Power, and Memory Wave 24

## Outcome

- New canonical settings: 10
- Existing canonical settings extended: 2
- New verified mappings: 31

- Remaining approved unmapped settings: 2858

Every decision uses visually reviewed official ASUS, ASRock, GIGABYTE, and MSI pages. Names, functions, options, dependencies, platform context, and menu hierarchy agree; no community source was required.

## Approved decisions

- **DF Cstates** (new identity) - 2 mappings from asrock, asus. Official ASRock and ASUS AMD manuals describe the same DF C-state firmware programming, Auto synchronization with Global C-state, and enable/disable choices.
- **L1 Burst Prefetch Mode** (new identity) - 2 mappings from asrock, asus. Official ASRock and ASUS AMD manuals use L1 Burst Prefetcher or L1 Burst Prefetch Mode and independently describe the same L1 burst-prefetch control with matching Auto, Disabled, and Enabled choices.
- **Restore AC Power Loss** (new identity) - 6 mappings from asrock, asus, msi. Official ASRock, ASUS, and MSI manuals independently document the same post-power-loss state control; wording varies between Restore on, Restore after, and Restore AC Power Loss.
- **Chipset Link Speed** (new identity) - 2 mappings from asrock, asus. Official ASRock and ASUS AMD manuals independently expose Auto and Gen1 through Gen4 for the CPU-to-chipset link; ASUS calls the control Chipset Link Mode.
- **DRAM VDD Switching Frequency** (new identity) - 3 mappings from asus, msi. Official ASUS and MSI Intel manuals independently identify the DRAM VDD PMIC regulator switching-frequency control.
- **DRAM VDDQ Switching Frequency** (new identity) - 3 mappings from asus, msi. Official ASUS and MSI Intel manuals independently identify the DRAM VDDQ PMIC regulator switching-frequency control.
- **DRAM VPP Switching Frequency** (new identity) - 3 mappings from asus, msi. Official ASUS and MSI Intel manuals independently identify the DRAM VPP PMIC regulator switching-frequency control.
- **Wake From Thunderbolt(TM) Devices** (new identity) - 3 mappings from asus, msi. Official MSI and ASUS Intel manuals independently document the same system-wake control under Thunderbolt configuration.
- **Intel(R) Turbo Boost Max Technology 3.0** (new identity) - 3 mappings from asus, msi. Official MSI and ASUS Intel manuals independently expose the same Intel Turbo Boost Max Technology 3.0 enable/disable control and processor-support dependency.
- **LCLK Frequency Control** (new identity) - 2 mappings from asus, msi. Official MSI and ASUS AMD manuals independently define Auto/default and Manual LCLK frequency control and gate the same Maximum Frequency child setting.
- **Intel(R) Speed Shift Technology** (existing identity extension) - 1 mapping from gigabyte. The GIGABYTE Intel 600 source has the same label and verbatim function text as an already mapped GIGABYTE source, while the existing identity is independently supported by ASUS official manuals.
- **HD Audio Controller** (existing identity extension) - 1 mapping from gigabyte. The GIGABYTE Intel 600 Audio Controller record has the same onboard-audio enable/disable function and verbatim explanatory text as already mapped GIGABYTE HD Audio Controller records, with independent MSI and ASUS support.

## Intentional exclusion

Processor CC drive strengths and Processor CKI drive strengths remain unmapped. The official pages expose separate CK, CC/CKI, CS, and CA controls, but do not establish that CC and CKI are equivalent. TuneForge does not infer the relationship.
